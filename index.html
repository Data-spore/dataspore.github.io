The Challenge

Enterprise adoption of AI agents in business operations is bottlenecked by fragmented SaaS data infrastructure. 88% of enterprise AI pilots fail in production because LLMs are fed bloated, raw JSON context dumps—creating unsustainable token costs, high latency, and severe privacy risks across cross-departmental workflows.

Dataspore OS serves as an agentic AI facilitator, replacing raw context dumps with pre-computed, deterministic data contracts that keep sensitive data strictly within your perimeter while empowering non-technical business leaders.

Core Pillars

  • Identity Hub (The "Who"): Unifies disjointed customer and operational touchpoints across department silos under a canonical Dataspore_ID.
  • Event Ledger (The "What"): High-speed, immutable event store providing auditability and context history for autonomous agents.
  • Metric Layer (The "Truth"): Pre-computed, machine-readable analytical views designed specifically for downstream LLM agents in Marketing, Sales, Operations, and Finance.
