The Opportunity

Enterprise adoption of AI agents is bottlenecked by data infrastructure. 88% of enterprise AI pilots fail in production because LLMs are fed bloated, raw JSON context dumps—creating unsustainable token costs, high latency, and severe privacy risks.

Dataspore OS replaces context dumps with pre-computed, deterministic data contracts, keeping sensitive PII strictly within the customer's perimeter.
